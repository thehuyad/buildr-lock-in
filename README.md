# BUILDR Lock In

BUILDR is a community for founders, entrepreneurs and builders. Twice a year 15 members of the BUILDR community are selected for a bootcamp called LOCK IN, where the selected member leave their houses to go to the camp where feeding and other necessities(accomodation, electricity, internet, etc) are taken care of for free and they stay there for 2weeks and do nothing but build for the entirety of the 2weeks. I want you to build a website for BUILDR showcasing all these, it should then have a CTA to register for the next LOCK IN or join the waitlist for it. i've uploaded some images of BUILDR. The brand colours are black, red, white and ash(you can see it in the BUILDR logo I uploaded.) also use the design principles and typography of the logo to create the website, it should feel VERY calm with A LOT of whitespaces.
